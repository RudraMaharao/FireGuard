Set up your home.

Connect FireGuard to your home Wi-Fi, add the location, and choose the residents who should receive alerts. Changes are sent to your connected ESP32.

Configure Wi-Fi, Telegram and the device connection through the ESP32 setup hotspot.

02 Home & emergency details
Find nearby fire stations ↗Uses your postal code, city and country in Maps. Choose a station and verify its details before saving.

03 Residents & their addresses

FireGuard
Experimental prototype. Keep certified fire, gas and CO alarms installed. Scores are relative indicators, not gas concentrations. Telegram needs power, home Wi-Fi and internet. Emergency forwarding is off by default and requires a Telegram recipient who has joined your bot. Phone numbers can be set to test numbers; no phone calls are made.
