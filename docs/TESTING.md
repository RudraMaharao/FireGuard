# Remaining physical checks - revision 2

User-reported bench passes: MQ divider/filter DC tests; ADS and BME supplies 3.29 V; transistor, diode and button tests; USB-C breakout output 5.11 V in the tested orientation. These do not validate the new assembled solder joints or the firmware on the board. The LM2596 and relay tests are now irrelevant because those parts are removed.

1. With all power removed, inspect polarity, joint bridges and ground continuity. Recheck the finished power rails and both USB-C plug orientations with a meter before powering sensitive boards. Do not guess an unverified transistor lead order.
2. Power from the single tested 5 V source. Expected: approximately 5 V main rail, approximately 3.3 V ADS/BME supply. For each divider, output is approximately half its actual input. A 5.00 V test input gives about 2.50 V. ADS inputs must remain within the 3.3 V supply rails even though the configured PGA spans +/-4.096 V.
3. Boot firmware. Verify revision `two-mq-ads1115-v2`. ADS address=0x48; BME chip ID=0x60 at 0x76 (fallback 0x77). ADS A0/A2 input display normally spans 0-2500 mV for 0-5000 mV module AO. The AO reading in the dashboard is twice ADC mV. There is no universal clean-air AO voltage for an MQ module.
4. While powered OFF, unplug an MQ AO or the ADS module, then reboot. Expected: input/ADC fault, no stale chemical score counted as healthy, fault notice after warm-up. Restore with power off. Confirm the BME missing-sensor fault similarly. Disconnected floating inputs can pick up voltage; a plausible ADC value alone cannot prove a sensor is physically connected.
5. Confirm buttons: B1 mute, B2 Telegram readings, B2 hold setup, B3 save fresh scored frame / hold test alert. Verify active buzzer on/off without heating the transistor. Keep total buzzer current within its rated 20 mA selection.
6. Confirm safe sensor/thermal stimuli wake the camera; check a fresh image and CNN timestamps. A stale camera image must not be reported as current. Check supply stability, sample cadence, free heap and task stacks while inference and HTTPS run together.
7. Register test Telegram resident and emergency recipient. Verify readings, test alert, acknowledgement and disabled forwarding. Then enable forwarding in TEST mode and test the 60 s unattended-warning policy using simulated/test input. No emergency department is contacted without a registered recipient.
8. Link AWS and verify a real hardware snapshot replaces the waiting state. Confirm queued settings apply and report a result. Power down the board: dashboard should show offline/last known readings after about 25 s.
9. Configure one SMS provider. Send its explicit test to your own number and verify receipt. API success only means accepted by the provider. Check disabled mode, invalid key/phone, provider errors and rate limits. Neither provider sends without internet; textbee also needs its Android phone online with a working SIM.
10. Collect labelled clean-air, cooking/steam and other safe representative scenes. Quantify false alarms and misses before tuning thresholds. No real-fire/CO performance or accuracy is established by the software tests.

Hybrid-location update: no new wiring. Save/verify fixed coordinates; optional Google Wi-Fi fallback and station-number SMS are configured on the local setup page. See LOCATION_SETUP.md (in docs/) for keys, defaults, freshness rules and station/test-number delivery. Current release binaries include these features.
